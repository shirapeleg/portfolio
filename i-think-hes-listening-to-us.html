Book

I THINK HE'S LISTENING TO US

For the past three years, journalist and literary critic Yuval Plotkin has been gathering fragments of conversations from the public sphere. unfiltered, personal, and amusing pieces of reality that were never meant for strangers' ears, and certainly not for public consumption.

In the book "I Think He's Listening to Us," I have selected about 170 quotes organized by theme, providing them with a visual interpretation through line and form. Ironically, out of this collection of anonymous quotes a broader picture of life in Israel in recent years is woven: what truly occupies our minds, the essential and the trivial and the endless necessity of humor.

320 pages, 16*19.5 cm, digital black marker, indigo print, hebrew, 2026
